Privacy Terms & EULA Support

Swiss Hike is a hiking app for Switzerland. It has no account, no login, no advertising, and no analytics. This page explains exactly what the app does with data — and what it deliberately does not do.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never learn who you are.
  • We collect nothing. No personal data is sent to us, because there is no “us” server to send it to.
  • No advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, no tracking, no device or advertising identifiers.
  • Everything you create in the app — favourites, notes, journals, photos, recorded hikes, plans — stays on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes that data.

1. Who is responsible

Swiss Hike (“the app”) is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). For anything in this policy, write to alice.turcanu91@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect about you

None. The app does not create a user profile on any server, does not send us usage statistics or crash reports of its own, and contains no advertising or analytics libraries. We cannot identify you, contact you, or see how you use the app unless you choose to email us.

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple gives us anonymous, aggregated sales and crash statistics through App Store Connect. Those reports are produced by Apple, never identify individual users, and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps your content locally, using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and files in the app’s own sandbox). It is never uploaded. This includes:

WhatWhere it lives
Favourites, collections, tags, saved searches, checklists, gear lists, goals and milestonesLocal app storage
Notes, journal entries, trip plans, scheduled hikes and custom waypointsLocal app storage
Recorded hikes (the GPS track of a walk you chose to record)Files in the app’s sandbox
Photos you attach to a hike’s photo logCopied as JPEGs into the app’s sandbox
Offline map tiles and cached trail, weather and elevation data you downloaded for a routeFiles in the app’s sandbox
Settings and preferencesLocal app storage

If you have iCloud Backup or an encrypted device backup enabled, iOS may include this app data in that backup. That is a feature of your device and your Apple Account, handled by Apple under their terms — the data still never reaches us.

4. Location

Location access is optional, and the app only asks after you have opened a trail and it can explain why. If you grant it, your location is used to:

  • open on or sort by the trail nearest you, and show how far the trailhead is;
  • show your position on the map while you follow a route;
  • measure distance, ascent and pace while you are actively recording a hike, and drive the Live Activity and off-route alerts.

The app requests “While Using the App” permission and uses the background-location capability only while a recording is running, so the walk keeps recording with the screen off. iOS shows the blue location indicator the whole time this happens. The app cannot locate you when no recording is in progress.

Your location is never transmitted to us and never shared with any third party for advertising or profiling. Coordinates are sent to the public data services in section 6 only to answer a request you made — for example the weather at a point, the map tiles for an area, or the next departure from a nearby stop.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. The rest of the app keeps working.

5. Other device permissions

  • Photos — only when you pick a picture for a hike’s photo log. The app reads the picture you selected and copies it into its own storage. It never scans your library.
  • Apple Health — write-only, and only when you tap “Export to Health” on a hike you recorded. The app writes that workout and never reads anything back from Health. Health data is stored by Apple on your device and never reaches us.
  • Notifications — local reminders only (for a hike you scheduled, or an alert during a walk). They are generated on your device; there are no push notifications from a server.
  • Motion & compass — used on-device for heading and step-based pace while the map or tracker is open, never stored or transmitted.
  • Siri & widgets — the Siri shortcut, home-screen widget and Live Activity read the data already on your device. Apple may process a spoken request under its own privacy policy.

6. Network requests

The app fetches maps and live conditions directly from public, key-free services. These requests carry only the coordinates or trail identifiers needed to answer them — no account identifier, no advertising identifier, no personal data:

  • geo.admin.ch (api3.geo.admin.ch, wmts.geo.admin.ch) — swisstopo and SwitzerlandMobility / ASTRA: trail routes, closures, place names, elevation profiles and map tiles.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) — weather forecasts and historical weather, based on MeteoSwiss data.
  • transport.opendata.ch — Swiss public-transport connections and departures.

Like any web request, these carry your IP address to the service you are contacting. We do not operate those services and are not responsible for their own data practices; consult each provider’s terms for detail. Where the app offers a link to an operator’s own website, a webcam page, or Apple Maps, tapping it hands you to that site or app under its own policy.

7. Purchases

The app may offer optional paid features or subscriptions. Any purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no Apple Account credentials. The app only learns, from Apple’s on-device receipt, whether a purchase is currently active, and that check happens between your device and Apple. Purchase and billing data is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

8. Sharing and exporting

Nothing leaves the app unless you send it. When you export a GPX track, share a hike, or write a backup file of your data, iOS’s share sheet puts it wherever you choose — Files, Mail, another app. That destination’s own policy then applies. We receive no copy.

9. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 16, because it does not collect personal data at all.

10. Your rights

Data-protection law (the Swiss FADP and the EU GDPR) gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. Your own copy is under your control: everything is on your device, exportable from within the app, and removed completely when you delete the app.
